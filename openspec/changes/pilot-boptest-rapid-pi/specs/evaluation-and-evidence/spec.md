# Exploratory closed-loop pilot
## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: BPI-001 Closed-loop development evidence boundaries
The pilot SHALL execute real official FMU transitions and distinguish predictive identification, control performance and calibration budgets.
#### Scenario: Reporting a pilot result
- **WHEN** a controller comparison is reported
- **THEN** it SHALL retain every development case, fixed and tuned PI comparators, simulator provenance, actuator bounds and calibration exposure
- **AND** it SHALL not claim official KPI equivalence, independent confirmation, human-time savings, neural improvement, humidity control or semiconductor validation
