# Research questions

RQ-BPI-02: How do 2 h and 6 h identification compare against fixed PI and a 9-candidate tuning grid?
C-BPI-01: Feasibility only, not novelty or demonstrated human-time savings. Simulated process exposure, wall time and number of trials are distinct.
