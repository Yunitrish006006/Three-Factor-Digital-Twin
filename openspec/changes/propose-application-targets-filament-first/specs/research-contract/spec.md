# Candidate application portfolio

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: APP-001 Candidate application portfolio

Reports SHALL list filament, wood and chassis applications as proposed; filament SHALL be identified as the user's exploration preference, not as a validated control application or proven best research direction.

#### Scenario: Professor reviews candidate applications

- **WHEN** the report displays the three targets
- **THEN** it distinguishes existing bounded estimation evidence from unevaluated interventions and product quality
- **AND** fixed-target conventional control is an explicit competitor, material moisture is distinct from RH, and heated filament drying is outside the original air-state domain
- **AND** the deliverable is offline HTML with traceable sources, not a new PPTX
