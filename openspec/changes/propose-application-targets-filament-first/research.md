# Exploratory questions

- RQ-APP-01: Does filament preservation need more than fixed targets with desiccant or ordinary feedback? Fixed targets are not fixed actuator outputs.
- RQ-APP-02: Can a physics plus small residual model estimate material state or drying completion beyond simple baselines with independent reference measurements?
- RQ-APP-03: Which application has identifiable actions, accessible ground truth and a useful product endpoint?

All questions are exploratory, NOT_EVALUATED. No confirmatory improvement threshold or novelty claim is asserted. Existing BMC/AAU evidence cannot be relabeled as material moisture, filament yield or control evidence. Environmental RH and material moisture differ. Confounders include material lot, printer settings, spool conditioning, storage enclosure, post-print conditioning and batch dependence. No personal-data collection is planned.
