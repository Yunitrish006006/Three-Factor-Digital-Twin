# Research Questions and Hypothesis

## RQ-ENC-04

Does a fixed sensor-role partition improve leave-one-sensor-out temperature reconstruction over a role-agnostic global mean on an independent AAU Server Room v4 byte-range sample?

## H-ENC-04

The role-conditioned mean has lower aggregate MAE and RMSE than the global mean, wins per-sensor MAE for at least 26 of 42 eligible sensors, and has a positive paired MAE-improvement 95% day-block bootstrap confidence interval.

## Rationale

Rack fronts face cold aisles while rack backs face hot aisles; their measurements therefore represent different thermal functions. The role map is inherited from the already frozen E11C metadata artifact, not selected from E11D outcomes.
