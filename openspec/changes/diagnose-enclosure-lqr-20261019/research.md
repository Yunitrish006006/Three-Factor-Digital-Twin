# Research questions and predeclared gates

RQ-LDA-1: Under true state, does replacing the fitted controller model with exact same-family plant parameters improve tracking? H-ENC-91 requires macro tracking MAE improvement >=5%, at least 6/8 paired wins, no higher mean sampled overtemperature, and no controller fallback.

RQ-LDA-2: With the fitted controller model fixed, does true state in place of sparse estimated state meet the same gate? H-ENC-92 uses the same conditions. These are descriptive exploratory gates; eight pairs on four plants are not eight independent devices and no significance or general causal law is claimed.

Report both conditional contrasts in the full factorial plus the difference-in-differences interaction. Retain PI, null/adverse outcomes, limits, failures and every pair. Fixed weights and shared limiting remain; raw requests and limiting are diagnostic counts, not an intervention on saturation. Oracle arms are simulation-only information interventions, not realizable sparse sensing. Same-family model truth excludes structural mismatch, unknown heat bias and physical validation; it cannot prove the cause of the earlier 10/12 ranking.
