# Startup handover factors
## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: SHA-001 Duration and handover SHALL be isolated
The experiment SHALL use identical pre-handover control at fixed duration and freeze selection before confirmation.
#### Scenario: Two handover modes are compared
- **WHEN** the same-duration pair has not triggered withdrawal
- **THEN** commanded inputs and temperatures SHALL be equal
- **AND** all subsequent early/late differences and adverse gates SHALL remain visible
