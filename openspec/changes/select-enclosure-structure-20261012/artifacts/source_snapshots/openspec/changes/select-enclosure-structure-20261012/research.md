# Research questions and exploratory criteria

- RQ-ENC-71: Can a common limited-data selection procedure identify when independent capacities and an extra thermal path improve observable prediction?
- RQ-ENC-72: Does that observable-only selection improve hidden-source estimation on fresh synthetic evaluation, including adverse cases?
- RQ-ENC-73: How does changing only the model/observer affect identical PI and six-step action-ranking mechanisms?
- H-ENC-71: on open-loop holdout, selected-corrected source MAE <=90% of common-capacity-corrected MAE, with wins in >=4/6 rig-seed pairs.
- H-ENC-72: on closed-loop holdout, selected H6 reduces tracking MAE >=5% versus common-capacity H6, without higher mean sampled overtemperature or >20% extra fan energy proxy.

Neither gate implies hardware efficacy or population generality. Observable model selection may prefer a model whose hidden source is worse; seven fitted parameters may be weakly identifiable. Record parameter-bound hits, Jacobian condition, failed fits, trial and CPU cost. Capacity ratios/contact/heat-path confounding and calibration/evaluation mismatch remain alternative explanations. No personal data; validation truth is quarantined from selection and control.
