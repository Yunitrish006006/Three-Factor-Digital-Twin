# Bounded Thermal Structure Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: ENC-701 Balanced versioned thermal candidate

The candidate SHALL expose independent heat capacities and an optional source-air path without modifying frozen study code or taking source-reference truth as input.

#### Scenario: Adding the path

- **WHEN** source-air conduction is enabled
- **THEN** the internal exchange SHALL be equal and opposite and leave total stored-energy balance intact

### Requirement: ENC-702 Observable commissioning selection

Structure selection SHALL use chronological commissioning inputs and a common fixed budget, retaining saturation/failure and all costs.

#### Scenario: Selecting a complex model

- **WHEN** F is considered
- **THEN** it SHALL meet the predeclared observed-data score rule without source truth or test outcomes
- **AND** the final choice SHALL not be refitted on opened evaluations

### Requirement: ENC-703 Fresh bounded evaluation

Fresh synthetic evaluation SHALL preserve previous evidence, complete all registered comparisons and independently audit selections and traces.

#### Scenario: An adverse result

- **WHEN** a hypothesis fails or an individual rig worsens
- **THEN** its result SHALL remain visible and SHALL not trigger unregistered gain or seed adjustments

### Requirement: SYN-701 HTML research delivery

The professor HTML SHALL distinguish earlier and new exploratory results and remain synchronized with applicable scientific artifacts.

#### Scenario: Delivering an update

- **WHEN** HTML changes
- **THEN** fresh responsive/offline/interaction/print observation SHALL be tied to its exact bytes
- **AND** Office rendering SHALL not become a report-completion gate
