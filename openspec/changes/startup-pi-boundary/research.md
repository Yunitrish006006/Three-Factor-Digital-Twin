# SPB-001 Research question
Can temporary bounded startup assistance improve first-hour error/target acquisition while preserving subsequent PI quality? Separate controller-law identity after handover from equality of physical trajectories. Manual device-specific selection forbidden; automatic selection over the same three bands is allowed. H-SPB-01: forwarded policies satisfy early improvement and late retention on both new dates. Fallbacks, missed targets and reversals remain visible. All five devices are development plants, not independent real industrial equipment.

Related implementation concept: MathWorks, Bumpless Control Transfer Between Manual and PID Control, https://www.mathworks.com/help/simulink/slref/bumpless-control-transfer-between-manual-and-pid-control.html (tracking/integral state for transfer). This is documentation, not a new cited paper or proof of our startup policy. Heating-only capacity, fixed installation settings, warmup transients, model uncertainty and finite trials are confounders. Public simulation has no personal data.
