# Design

## One-Time Confirmation

E11F ranges were reserved before E11E and remained unrequested through E11H. The confirmation runner verifies the E11H hash and directly evaluates its frozen sensor map.

## Independence Boundary

Byte-level observations are disjoint. Calendar dates may overlap because all samples come from one AAU campaign. The result therefore measures unseen-record transfer under a fixed deployment, not temporal domain shift or a second enclosure.

## Failure Handling

Null, adverse, missing, and malformed outcomes remain in evidence. No E11F model revision is allowed. Any follow-up model must use a newly declared development source.
