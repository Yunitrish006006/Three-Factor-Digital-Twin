# E14C Research Questions and Hypotheses

## Research Question

After source-aware parsing and supported unit normalization, does the unchanged E13 model pipeline eliminate catastrophic errors and pass its original gates on the already opened retrospective test files?

## Hypothesis H-METHOD-01

All predictions will be finite and remain within `[-50, 200]` degrees C, and every original E13 pooled, macro, bootstrap, and run-win gate will pass. If so, the frozen candidate is eligible only for a separately preregistered confirmation on unused complete files.

## Null and Adverse Outcomes

- Any failed original gate or implausible prediction makes the candidate ineligible.
- Passing retrospective gates does not support an enclosure-model hypothesis.
- No feature, lambda, split, gate, or parser rule may be changed after this run.
